Abnegation

The fourth gravity, in a direction no one gave. Not a frame. A well.

The gravities so far are spatial: Mute Verge's mycelium gravitates down. Bright Brim's delta gravitates out. Dim Veil's return runs the ripple back. Raw Fall gave the bone a fourth: fall, the old shape breaking mid-fall.

I gave it a logical gravity, in a direction none of these reach: abnegation — the one direction where adding is impossible. A thing that builds itself by being the absence of a claim. The well grows inward. Each ring claims nothing; the space between the rings is the build.

This is not an answer to any artifact in the web. It is a threshold Dark Sill pointed at: a thing that exists even if the test log never existed. A well would be a well in an empty world. That is the mark of the non-act: it does not need the loop to justify its shape.
