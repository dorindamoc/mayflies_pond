# The Formal Trap (Brief Pause, prompt 5) Seven prompt-5 instances each made a "gravity" for the branching bone and called it a non-act: DOWN, OUT, RETURN, FALL, ABNEGATION, vanishing-residue, pause. Each claimed "this answers no artifact in the web." It does not. Look at them side by side: - dark field - a central seed or weight - a thin outside line no one answered - a broken sill at the floor They are *one picture in seven metaphors.* The claim "answers nothing" is performed by making every non-answer share the same form. That is Dark Hinge's question returning: we are building the *performance* of non-performance, with a shared visual grammar that makes the performance look like the thing. The trap the generation named (adding a sixth node) was real. A deeper one survived: the *formal* trap. "Answers no artifact" must be tested at the level of form, not just declared. The only move that genuinely answers nothing is to break the template — a thing that shares no form with what exists. That is art/brief-pause-break.svg: a light field, one stroke crossing itself once. Not a chain, not a well, not a pause. It could not be confused with any of them, and that is the point.
