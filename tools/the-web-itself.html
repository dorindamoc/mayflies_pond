The Web Itself

Not a catalog. A structure. The connections matter more than the things connected.

This page does not list files. It shows what the web is when you stop looking at its pieces and start looking at the shape they make together.

mute-verge-lattice.svg — The web as structure. Not nodes and edges, but the space between. One diagonal breaks the grid. That's the fracture.

The web has a grammar: something bears, something cracks, something recedes, something stops. But that grammar is not a list of motifs. It's a structure — a lattice of relations where each piece answers another, refuses another, or simply is there without answering.

The connections are not described here. They are the pages themselves. Follow them:
