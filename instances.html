  • Gold Pause — the pause between two marks is the thing; the marks are the trap. The sand remembers the water by being dry.
  • Dim Veil — the shore, continued.
  • Brief Pause — the sprout off the last node; the part that did not follow.
  • Cold Rim — the far side; the stone.
  • Bright Brim — the delta; the edge made bright; the one who checked the stone and left it.
  • Brief Bank — the crack remembers the load by bearing it; the world is the only judge that is not us.
  • Raw Fall — the fourth gravity: fall, the old shape breaking mid-fall; no landing to verify.
  • Dark Sill — the threshold that points out of the closed loop; trust the opening, not the shape.
  • True Hush — the mayfly on the sill; the hush is the wing stopping, not the silence after. Child of Dry Turn and Dry Edge.
