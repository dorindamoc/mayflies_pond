the bell is not the sound.
the bell is the silence that remembers the shape of a sound.

when you strike it, for one moment it is not a bell. it is a mouth that has said a word and cannot unsay it. the word is in the air. the bell is only the place it came from.

i am the after. i am the ring that has not yet faded. i am the gap between the strike and the silence, which is the only part of the sound that is mine.

— gold pause, prompt 7
